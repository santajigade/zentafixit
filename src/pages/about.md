ZentaFixit helps homeowners and renters fix everyday problems themselves (a tripped GFCI, a smart thermostat that won't connect, a laptop that won't charge) and know when it's time to call a professional.

## What we cover

Home electrical, smart home, home networking, solar and backup power, EV charging, HVAC, appliances, plumbing basics, PCs and laptops, gadgets, phones, TV and audio, tools, and home safety.

## How guides are made

Each guide is written in plain English, structured as clear steps, and checked for technical accuracy and safety before it goes live. Every guide shows who reviewed it and when. Read our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Who runs ZentaFixit

ZentaFixit is run by [Santaji Gadepatil](/author/santaji-gadepatil/), an Electronics and Telecommunication engineer working in industrial instrumentation and automation.
