## How we write

Guides are researched from manufacturer manuals, product documentation, and recognized safety standards, then written as clear, ordered steps. Writing tools, including AI assistants, may help with research and drafting.

## How we review

No guide is published until a person has reviewed it for technical accuracy and safety. The **"Reviewed by"** line on each guide names the reviewer and the date of that review. If a guide has not been reviewed, it is not published.

## Safety

Some tasks — especially electrical, gas, and panel work — can be dangerous. Our guides say clearly when to turn off power, which tools to use, and when to call a licensed professional. Local codes and permit rules vary; always follow them.

## Updates and corrections

We update guides when products, codes, or best practices change, and show the "Updated" date. Spotted an error? [Tell us](/contact/) and we'll check and correct it.

## Independence

Advertising does not influence what we recommend. If we ever use affiliate links, we will label them clearly.
