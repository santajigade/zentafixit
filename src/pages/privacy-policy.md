This policy explains what information ZentaFixit ("we") collects when you visit zentafixit.com and how it is used.

## Information we collect

We do not require accounts and do not ask for personal information to read our guides. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, used only to reply.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools may automatically collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, and referring site. This helps us understand how the site is used and keep it secure.

## Cookies and advertising

We may use cookies for analytics and advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA or the EU/UK under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, delete, or limit the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request.

## Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date above.
